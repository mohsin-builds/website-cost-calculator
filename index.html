What determines the cost of a website?

Two websites can look similar at first glance but require very different amounts of work. These are some of the main factors that influence the final project scope.

01

Number of pages

Larger websites require more layouts, content preparation and development.

02

Design complexity

Premium custom interfaces require more design work than simple or template-led layouts.

03

Features & integrations

Booking systems, payments, member areas and other functionality increase development complexity.

04

Content & SEO

Copywriting, page structure and search optimisation can add significant value and additional project work.

The calculator provides a planning range based on the requirements you select. It is intended as a useful starting point rather than a fixed quotation.

What affects the cost of a website? +

Website cost is influenced by page count, design complexity, e-commerce functionality, booking systems, payments, copywriting, SEO and integrations.

Why does an e-commerce website usually cost more? +

E-commerce projects typically require product management, shopping carts, checkout, payments and store configuration, which increases the overall scope.

Is the calculator result a fixed Mohsin Builds quote? +

No. The result is a planning estimate. A final Mohsin Builds quote depends on the confirmed project scope, content, integrations and specific requirements.
