What determines the cost of a website?

Two websites can look similar at first glance but require very different amounts of work. These are some of the biggest factors that influence the final project scope.

01

Number of pages

Larger websites require more layouts, content preparation and development.

02

Design complexity

Premium custom interfaces require more design work than template-based or simple layouts.

03

Features & integrations

Booking systems, payments, member areas and other functionality increase development complexity.

04

Content & SEO

Copywriting, page structure and search optimisation can add significant value and additional project work.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Website cost calculator FAQ

A few important things to know before planning your website project.

How accurate is this website cost calculator? +

The calculator provides a professional planning range based on the project requirements you select. It is intended as a starting estimate rather than a fixed quotation.

What affects the cost of a website? +

Website cost is influenced by factors such as the number of pages, design complexity, e-commerce functionality, booking systems, payments, copywriting, SEO and other integrations.

Why does an e-commerce website usually cost more? +

E-commerce projects generally require additional functionality including product management, shopping carts, checkout, payment processing and store configuration.

Is the calculator result a fixed Mohsin Builds quote? +

No. The result is a planning estimate. A final Mohsin Builds quote would depend on the confirmed project scope, content, integrations and specific requirements.

How long does it take to build a website? +

Timelines vary depending on the size and complexity of the project. A focused landing page can be completed much faster than a large e-commerce website or custom platform. The calculator provides an estimated build time based on your selections.
